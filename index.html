Exact guest process

From RSVP to doors open

The same rhythm the world's best event platforms use, rewritten for Bangladesh local addresses. Times lock to each event’s start clock (Asia/Dhaka).

  1. 00:00

    Register

    Pick a ticket, enter name, email, phone and city. Instant “you’re in” or waitlist if the room is full.

  2. T-7d

    Calendar hold

    Google Calendar and .ics stay attached. Host sees a frozen yes-list starting seven days out.

  3. T-24h

    Address unlock

    The exact local pin (house, road, cafe) is revealed on your ticket. Travel notes for your city appear.

  4. T-3h

    Leave window

    OPEN BOX shows when to leave, based on Dhaka / Chattogram traffic windows, plus a host contact.

  5. T-60m

    Final ping

    One-hour reminder. WhatsApp circle link. Last chance to drop so a waitlisted guest can take the seat.

  6. T-15m

    Check-in QR live

    Show the QR at the door. Hosts scan. Late check-in stays open until T+10 minutes.

  7. T-5m

    Doors

    Last call. Seats not checked in can be released to the waitlist.

  8. T-0

    Meet starts

    Host opens. After close, attendees rate the host. Scores feed division rankings and OpenAI influencer badges.
